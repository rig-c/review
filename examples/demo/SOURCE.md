# Abstract lantern source study

The SVG, synthetic canonical pose sequence coordinates, and short synthetic sine-wave chime were authored for this repository. They contain no imported or private art.

This package carries a minimal synthetic compiled rig (`skeleton.model.json` and `lantern.png`) with no producer handoff, opened with `--view-only`. It demonstrates playback inspection, source comparison, checkpoint notes, audio audition, explicit review status, and structured frame/target/category feedback. Producer rebuild panels become available when a package is opened with its producer handoff (`config.json`, `parts.json`, `rig/`). The pose sequence demonstrates the canonical `rig-pose-sequence/1` transport shape; its coordinates were authored here and were not extracted or measured from footage. This synthetic demo is not proof of upstream compile gating or rig quality.
