# Abstract lantern source study

The SVG, synthetic canonical pose sequence coordinates, and short synthetic sine-wave chime were authored for this repository. They contain no imported or private art.

This package deliberately has no compiled rig. It demonstrates source comparison, checkpoint notes, audio audition, explicit review status, and structured frame/target/category feedback. Playback becomes available when an agent supplies `model` pointing to a gated rigc build's `skeleton.model.json`, with its local page images. The pose sequence demonstrates the canonical `rig-pose-sequence/1` transport shape; its coordinates were authored here and were not extracted by Nova or measured from footage. This source sketch is not proof of rig quality.
