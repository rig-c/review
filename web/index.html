                          REQUIREMENTS · RIG-PARTS CHECK
                          Declare what the scene asks of the motion: a slot (picked on the stage), its mesh or a bone, an animation and a kind, with your own bar. Check writes a spine-parts-requirements/1 file into scratch and runs the installed producer's rig-parts check --requirements on the inspected rig or the latest scratch build; each requirement gets one line with its worst frame (click to seek). A refusal is the producer's, by name. Waivers (may_fold), scene targets and the part exception lists are not written here. Submitted feedback carries the file by hash and content.
                          RETARGET · RIG-POSE
                          Put a pose sequence the package declares onto this rig: edit the rig-pose-map/1 (per chain, the bone that carries each segment), set the clean and retarget flags, and Apply. The installed @rig-c/pose runs rig-pose clean and rig-pose retarget onto the handoff rig, and its rigc compiles the result, all into scratch; the new animation plays here beside the reference (pick the sequence under Reference). Review writes no keys and never writes the package's motion.json. Refusals (POSE_NOT_CLEANED, POSE_BEND_ONE_WAY, RAISED_HEAD, MOTION_NAME_FREE, MAP_*) are the producer's, verbatim. FEET, STANCE, FLIP, SHORT, END-ON, LEG-SCALE and REACH lines seek to their frame.
